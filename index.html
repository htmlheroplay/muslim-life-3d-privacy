Privacy Policy

Muslim Life 3D • Updated September 2026

Welcome to Muslim Life 3D ("the Game", "we", "our", or "us"), developed by HTMLHERO PLAY. We value your trust and are committed to safeguarding your privacy. This page explains our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our mobile game.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect or store any Personally Identifiable Information (PII). You can play the game fully without providing any personal details:

  • No account creation or login required.
  • No access to personal photos, contacts, camera, or microphone.
  • No real-time GPS location tracking.
  • All game progress and earned rewards are stored locally on your own device.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the game completely free to play, we display non-intrusive advertisements powered by Google AdMob. Third-party advertising partners like AdMob may automatically collect anonymous, technical data such as:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID)
  • Device model, manufacturer, and operating system
  • Coarse geographic location (country/city derived from IP address)
  • Ad interaction logs and performance diagnostics

This technical data is used solely to deliver relevant advertisements and prevent ad fraud. You can learn more about how Google uses your data by visiting Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Permissions Used

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Allows the game to load advertisements via Google AdMob and verify network connectivity.
  • Local Storage: Strictly used to save your current mission level, settings, and player progress on your device.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Muslim Life 3D is designed for a general audience aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that your child has provided us with any personal data, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly remove it.

5. Security

Because your game progress is stored locally on your device, you retain full control of your save data. All communication between our advertising partners and ad servers is encrypted using standard HTTPS/TLS protocols.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We advise you to review this page periodically for any updates.
